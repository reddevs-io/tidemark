Formatter output conventions

RationaleMatches the plan examples and locale rules.
Detailssteps() returns the bare number; bpm and durations come from string resources (7 h 32 min); times follow the device 12/24-hour setting; ICU for times and day/month labels, java.time for SHORT/MEDIUM/LONG dates; distance 2 decimals below 1, 1 below 100, else 0; mass 1 decimal; energy and heart rate 0.
