Allowed non-Health permission from androidx

RationaleIt grants no access to anything outside the app and can't be removed without breaking androidx.core.
DetailsThe APK requests io.reddevs.tidemark.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, an app-private signature permission that androidx.core adds for non-exported dynamic receivers. ManifestContractTest allows it, and the T-008 CI permission check must allow it too.
