Tidemark product brief

DetailsTidemark (applicationId io.reddevs.tidemark, company domain reddevs.io) is an open-source, read-only Android viewer for Health Connect data. It has a dashboard with trends and a record explorer for raw records. License GPL-3.0. Repo github.com/reddevs-io/tidemark. Data never leaves the device: no INTERNET permission, no backend, no sync. English and French from v1. Distribution: GitHub Releases first (Obtainium-friendly), Google Play later under the existing reddevs Organization developer account.
