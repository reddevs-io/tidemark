Onboarding and privacy rationale

DetailsChecks Health Connect availability with getSdkStatus. On Android 9-13 Health Connect is a separate Play app: manifest needs a <queries> entry for com.google.android.apps.healthdata and the app shows an install prompt. Runs the permission request flow and handles partial grants. Required rationale handling, without which the permission dialog won't show: an activity for androidx.health.ACTION_SHOW_PERMISSIONS_RATIONALE (Android 13 and below) and an activity-alias for android.intent.action.VIEW_PERMISSION_USAGE with category android.intent.category.HEALTH_PERMISSIONS, protected by android.permission.START_VIEW_PERMISSION_USAGE (Android 14+). Both show the bundled in-app privacy policy.
