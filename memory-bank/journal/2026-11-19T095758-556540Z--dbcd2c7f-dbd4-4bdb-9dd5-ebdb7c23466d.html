Chart bucket rules

RationaleKeeps each chart to one aggregate call and attributes overnight sleep to a single morning.
DetailsDay range: hourly buckets via aggregateGroupByDuration. Week and month: daily buckets; year: monthly buckets via aggregateGroupByPeriod. Year view of cumulative metrics shows the daily average per month over days with data. Weeks start per WeekFields.of(locale). Sleep buckets run noon to noon.
