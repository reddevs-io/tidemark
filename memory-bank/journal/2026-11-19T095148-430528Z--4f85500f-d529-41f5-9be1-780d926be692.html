Play Health apps requirements (research)

DetailsPlay listing needs: the Health apps declaration with a justification for each data type in EN and FR; a Data safety form; a privacy policy URL that matches the in-app rationale; every permission tied to a visible feature (Play denies bulk permission lists when not necessary); history permission justified as view your past records. Review time is undocumented, unofficially 1-2 weeks, and any change to the type set means re-declaring. Other connect-client apps ship on F-Droid, possibly with a NonFreeDep anti-feature on Android 13 and below.
