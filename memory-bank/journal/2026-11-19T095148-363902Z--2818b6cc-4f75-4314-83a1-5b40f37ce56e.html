Dashboard

DetailsTiles for steps, active calories, distance, last night's sleep with stages, resting heart rate and latest weight, each with a sparkline. A tile opens a detail chart with day/week/month/year ranges. The chart drills down to that day's records in the explorer. Charts use Health Connect aggregates (aggregate, aggregateGroupByPeriod, aggregateGroupByDuration for the day view), which deduplicate by the user's data-source priority in Health Connect settings. No per-metric source picker.
